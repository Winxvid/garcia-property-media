Production Pipeline

From Shoot to Screen

01

Book & Brief

Fill out our quote form with the property details, desired package, and preferred shoot date. We'll confirm within hours and provide a pre-shoot checklist to ensure everything is ready.

02

On-Site Capture

We arrive, scout the space, and shoot. Mobile-first, agile, and respectful of the property. Most shoots wrap in 1–2 hours with zero disruption to sellers or neighbors.

03

AI Enhancement

Footage is immediately processed through our AI pipeline — stabilization, motion enhancement, intelligent lighting correction, and cinematic framing. This happens before a single manual edit.

04

Edit & Grade

Our editors craft the final cut — music sync, color grading, text overlays, pacing, and transitions. Every element is intentional. Every second earns its place on screen.

05

Deliver & Post

Files are delivered via private download link within 24–48 hours, in all required formats. Ready to upload straight to MLS, Instagram, YouTube, or wherever your audience lives.
